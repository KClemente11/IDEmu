IDEmu Native boots iDrive 4 (CIC High), iDrive 6 (NBT Evo), iDrive 7 (MGU) and iDrive 8 (MGU22) directly on Windows. Choose iDrive 8 in New environment using the normal IDEmu Native shortcut. iDrive 7 needs a graphics driver with OpenGL 4.1. iDrive 8 uses 12 GiB of guest RAM and requires x64 ANGLE libraries; see docs/WINDOWS-ID8-PORT.md in the source repository for graphics setup and current limitations. The app looks for firmware under C:\BMW\PSdZData, and you can select another location in System Files.

Setup installs for your Windows account. Your firmware, environments, and settings are stored outside the application directory. The separate WSL2 edition can run its supported generations using its existing data folder.
